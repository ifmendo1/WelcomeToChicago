Explore Chicago During the Day

Chicago offers a variety of daytime activities for both visitors and locals. From world-class museums and exciting sporting events to beautiful parks and family-friendly attractions, there is always something to explore.

Museums and Cultural Attractions

Immerse yourself in Chicago's rich history, art, science, and culture at these must-visit destinations.

  • Art Institute of Chicago – Explore famous paintings, sculptures, and artwork from around the world.
  • Field Museum – Discover ancient civilizations, dinosaur fossils, and natural history.
  • Griffin Museum of Science and Industry – Experience interactive exhibits focused on science, technology, and innovation.
  • Chicago History Museum – Learn about the city's history, culture, and development.

Sports

Experience the energy of one of America's most passionate sports cities.

  • Wrigley Field – Visit the historic home of the Chicago Cubs in the lively Wrigleyville neighborhood.
  • United Center – Experience the excitement of a Chicago Bulls basketball game or a Chicago Blackhawks hockey game.
  • Soldier Field – Catch a Chicago Bears football game at this iconic stadium near Lake Michigan.
  • Rate Field – Enjoy a Chicago White Sox baseball game on the city's South Side.

Parks

Take a break from the busy city streets and discover some of Chicago's most beautiful green spaces.

  • Millennium Park – Visit the famous Bean sculpture, beautiful gardens, and outdoor art installations.
  • Grant Park – Explore Buckingham Fountain and enjoy beautiful views of the Chicago skyline.
  • Humboldt Park – Discover scenic lagoons, walking paths, and the neighborhood's rich Puerto Rican culture.
  • Ping Tom Memorial Park – Enjoy a peaceful waterfront park in Chinatown with unique views of the city.
  • Garfield Park Conservatory – Explore beautiful indoor gardens filled with tropical plants, flowers, and greenery.

Zoos and Wildlife

Get closer to nature and discover fascinating wildlife through these family-friendly attractions.

  • Lincoln Park Zoo – Enjoy free admission to one of the oldest zoos in the United States, located near the lakefront.
  • Brookfield Zoo Chicago – Discover wildlife from around the world at this large zoo in suburban Brookfield.
  • Shedd Aquarium – Explore aquatic life, including penguins, sharks, beluga whales, and colorful tropical fish.
  • Peggy Notebaert Nature Museum – Experience local wildlife, nature exhibits, and an indoor butterfly haven.
