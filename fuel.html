Discover the Flavors of Chicago

Chicago is a city full of flavor, offering everything from award-winning restaurants to cozy coffee shops and iconic local dishes. Whether you are looking for a quick bite or a memorable dining experience, there is something for every taste.

Restaurants

Explore Chicago's diverse culinary scene, from casual neighborhood favorites to unique dining experiences.

  • Au Cheval – Enjoy a popular burger destination in the West Loop with a vintage diner atmosphere.
  • Kasama – Discover Filipino-inspired cuisine and beautifully crafted pastries in Ukrainian Village.
  • Pequod's Pizza – Experience Chicago-style pan pizza known for its caramelized crust.
  • Girl & the Goat – Explore creative dishes and globally inspired flavors in the West Loop.
  • La Chaparrita Taqueria – Enjoy authentic Mexican street-style tacos in the Little Village neighborhood.
  • Cafecito – Discover Cuban-inspired sandwiches and traditional flavors in a casual setting.

Coffee Shops

Take a break from exploring the city and discover Chicago's creative coffee culture, from cozy neighborhood cafés to specialty coffee destinations.

  • Sawada Coffee – Discover an industrial-style coffee shop in the West Loop known for its signature Military Latte.
  • Metric Coffee – Experience locally roasted specialty coffee in Chicago's West Town neighborhood.
  • Wormhole Coffee – Visit a retro-inspired coffee shop in Wicker Park filled with nostalgic decorations.
  • La Colombe – Enjoy specialty espresso drinks and draft lattes in a modern café setting.
  • Intelligentsia Coffee – Discover a Chicago-founded coffee company known for its specialty coffee and carefully sourced beans.
  • Dark Matter Coffee – Explore a creative Chicago coffee brand known for its colorful artwork and distinctive coffee blends.

Local Delicacies

No visit to Chicago is complete without experiencing the iconic flavors that have helped shape the city's culinary identity.

  • Deep-Dish Pizza – Try this Chicago classic at Lou Malnati's or Giordano's.
  • Chicago-Style Hot Dog – Enjoy an all-beef hot dog with traditional toppings at Portillo's or Superdawg Drive-In.
  • Italian Beef Sandwich – Experience a flavorful Chicago staple at Al's Beef or Johnnie's Beef.
  • Chicago-Style Popcorn – Discover the sweet and savory combination of caramel and cheese popcorn at Garrett Popcorn Shops.
  • Jibarito – Try a Chicago-born Puerto Rican sandwich made with fried plantains instead of bread at Jibaritos y Más.
  • Rainbow Cone – Enjoy a colorful combination of five ice cream flavors at the Original Rainbow Cone.
