Relax and Recharge in Chicago

Sometimes the best way to experience Chicago is to slow down and enjoy the outdoors. From scenic trails and beautiful beaches to peaceful green spaces, the city offers plenty of opportunities to relax and recharge.

Hiking Trails

Escape the busy city streets and explore Chicago's scenic trails at your own pace.

  • The 606 – Explore an elevated recreational trail connecting several Chicago neighborhoods through art and green spaces.
  • Chicago Lakefront Trail – Enjoy scenic views of Lake Michigan and the city skyline while walking, running, or cycling.
  • North Branch Trail – Discover peaceful wooded paths and natural landscapes away from downtown.
  • Palos Trail System – Explore forested hiking and biking trails in the southwestern suburbs of Chicago.

Beaches

Enjoy a refreshing escape along Lake Michigan, from lively beachfront destinations to quieter spots perfect for relaxation.

  • North Avenue Beach – Enjoy beach volleyball, waterfront activities, and beautiful views of the Chicago skyline.
  • Oak Street Beach – Relax on a sandy beach surrounded by downtown Chicago's iconic skyscrapers.
  • Montrose Beach – Explore a spacious beach with picnic areas, a dog-friendly section, and nearby natural habitats.
  • Foster Beach – Enjoy a more laid-back beachfront experience in the Edgewater neighborhood.
  • 57th Street Beach – Discover a scenic South Side beach near Hyde Park and Jackson Park.

Active Lifestyle

Stay active while discovering Chicago through outdoor sports, recreational activities, and unique fitness experiences.

  • Maggie Daley Park – Enjoy rock climbing, miniature golf, and seasonal skating surrounded by downtown skyscrapers.
  • Lincoln Park Tennis Courts – Spend an afternoon playing tennis in one of Chicago's most scenic parks.
  • Chicago River Kayaking – Explore the city's famous architecture from a unique perspective on the water.
  • Divvy Bikes – Rent a bicycle and explore Chicago's neighborhoods, parks, and waterfront at your own pace.
  • Montrose Harbor – Enjoy outdoor exercise, jogging, and peaceful walks with beautiful lakefront views.

Nature

Reconnect with nature and discover peaceful green spaces tucked away in and around Chicago.

  • Lincoln Park Conservatory – Explore lush indoor gardens filled with tropical plants, ferns, and seasonal flowers.
  • North Park Village Nature Center – Discover peaceful walking paths, woodlands, wetlands, and native wildlife.
  • Montrose Point Bird Sanctuary – Enjoy birdwatching and scenic natural habitats along Lake Michigan.
  • Alfred Caldwell Lily Pool – Discover a tranquil landscape featuring stone pathways, native plants, and a beautiful pond.
  • Chicago Botanic Garden – Explore beautifully designed gardens, scenic lakes, and seasonal floral displays in suburban Glencoe.
