Explore Chicago After Dark

When the sun goes down, Chicago comes alive with vibrant nightlife, live music, and unique social experiences. From energetic nightclubs to relaxing cocktail lounges, there is something for everyone.

Nightclubs

Experience Chicago's energetic nightlife through its diverse music scene and dance floors.

  • Smartbar – Discover Chicago's underground electronic and house music scene in Wrigleyville.
  • Spybar – Enjoy electronic music in an intimate nightclub setting in River North.
  • TAO Chicago – Experience an upscale nightlife destination with elaborate interior design.
  • Primary NightClub – Explore a more intimate setting for electronic music and dancing.

Neighborhoods

Discover the unique personalities of Chicago's neighborhoods as the city comes alive after dark.

  • Boystown (Northalsted) – Explore Chicago's historic LGBTQ+ neighborhood, known for its welcoming community, lively nightlife, rainbow crosswalks, and Pride celebrations.
  • River North – Discover upscale lounges, rooftop bars, and energetic nightclubs.
  • West Loop – Enjoy stylish cocktail lounges, trendy restaurants, and vibrant evening entertainment.
  • Wicker Park – Experience an artistic neighborhood filled with independent music venues, creative bars, and eclectic entertainment.
  • Logan Square – Discover neighborhood cocktail bars, intimate music venues, and a relaxed local atmosphere.
  • Wrigleyville – Experience the energetic nightlife along Clark Street, filled with sports bars, live music, lively pubs, and entertainment beyond baseball season.

Bars

Unwind after a day of exploring with creative cocktails, beautiful skyline views, and lively local entertainment.

  • Sidetrack Video Bar – Visit an iconic LGBTQ+ bar in Boystown known for its music videos, themed parties, and lively atmosphere.
  • Cindy's Rooftop – Enjoy cocktails and panoramic views of Millennium Park and Lake Michigan.
  • Three Dots and a Dash – Discover a tropical-inspired cocktail bar tucked away in River North.
  • The Green Mill – Experience live jazz in a historic cocktail lounge in the Uptown neighborhood.
  • CloudBar at 360 CHICAGO – Enjoy panoramic skyline views and cocktails from the 94th floor of the former John Hancock Center. Observation deck admission is required.
