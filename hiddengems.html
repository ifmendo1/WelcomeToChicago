Discover Chicago's Hidden Gems

Beyond Chicago's famous attractions, there are plenty of unique places waiting to be discovered. Explore local shops, neighborhood favorites, and exciting activities that offer a different perspective of the city and its surrounding areas.

Shopping

Discover unique finds, vintage treasures, and distinctive shopping experiences beyond the typical tourist destinations.

  • Village Discount Outlet – Explore affordable secondhand clothing, accessories, and unexpected vintage finds.
  • Brown Elephant – Discover secondhand furniture, clothing, artwork, and home decor while supporting a charitable cause.
  • Lost Girls Vintage – Browse a curated selection of vintage clothing and unique fashion pieces.
  • Andersonville Galleria – Shop for handmade artwork, jewelry, home decor, and gifts from independent vendors.
  • 900 North Michigan Shops – Explore an upscale indoor shopping destination along the Magnificent Mile.
  • Fashion Outlets of Chicago – Discover designer brands and discounted merchandise in nearby Rosemont.
  • Randolph Street Market – Explore seasonal markets featuring antiques, vintage fashion, collectibles, and local artists.

Food

Discover unexpected culinary treasures, international flavors, and unique food markets throughout Chicago's diverse neighborhoods.

  • Chicago French Market – Explore an indoor marketplace featuring international cuisine, pastries, and specialty foods.
  • Joong Boo Market – Discover Korean groceries, traditional snacks, and freshly prepared dishes.
  • Chinatown Square – Explore authentic Chinese cuisine, bakeries, bubble tea shops, and specialty stores.
  • H Mart – Discover Asian groceries, international snacks, and Korean food specialties.
  • Margie's Candies – Enjoy homemade-style ice cream sundaes and chocolates in a nostalgic setting.
  • Maxwell Street Market – Explore a historic seasonal market featuring street food, local vendors, and cultural traditions.

Activities

Discover unforgettable experiences beyond Chicago's traditional tourist attractions, from creative urban adventures to scenic day trips.

  • The Forge: Lemont Quarries – Experience outdoor adventures, climbing, ziplining, and recreational activities in nearby Lemont.
  • Starved Rock State Park – Take a day trip near Utica, Illinois, to explore scenic hiking trails, sandstone canyons, seasonal waterfalls, and breathtaking views of the Illinois River.
  • Matthiessen State Park – Discover beautiful rock formations, wooded trails, and waterfalls near Starved Rock.
  • Chicago Architecture Center River Cruise – Discover the stories behind Chicago's iconic buildings from the Chicago River.
  • Chicago Cultural Center – Explore free art exhibitions, historic architecture, and stunning stained-glass domes.
  • Pullman National Historical Park – Discover a historic South Side neighborhood and learn about Chicago's industrial and architectural heritage.
